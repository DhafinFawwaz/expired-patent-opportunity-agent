Unresolved risks and next steps

Treat every candidate as a lead to check, not a product to build yet.

Unresolved risks

  • 15 related records (continuations, citations, same owner) need their own status check.
  • A lapsed patent can be reinstated; recent lapses carry the most risk.
  • Design patents and trade dress may cover the product's look.

Next steps

  1. Confirm each status in USPTO Patent Center and check for a pending petition.
